Built into every track

You'll know exactly what your learners gained

Every track includes a structured pre-assessment before training begins and a post-assessment when it ends. The delta between the two is documented, organized by cohort, and delivered as a skills gain report your institution can act on.

01
Pre-Assessment
Every learner establishes a role-specific AI readiness baseline before training begins. No generic quiz. Benchmarks tied to their sector and job context.
02
Scenario-Based Training
Hours of sector-specific, workflow-matched training across 8 to 9 modules. Delivered in whatever format works for your institution.
03
Post-Assessment and Skills Report
Learners are re-assessed and their skills gain is documented. Your institution receives a cohort-level report showing before and after results for every participant.
