Accreditation alignment

Accreditors are watching AI. Your documentation needs to be ready.

Regional accreditors are increasingly evaluating how institutions handle AI in their educational quality frameworks. SACSCOC issued formal guidance on AI in December 2024 and followed with Good Practices documentation in June 2025. HLC's revised Federal Compliance Requirements tighten expectations around institutional effectiveness, effective September 2026. Every AI Essentials deployment produces the documentation trail these frameworks are beginning to require.

SACSCOC issued AI guidance in December 2024. Programmatic accreditors including ABHES, ACCSC, and COE have all incorporated technology governance questions into their evaluation criteria.
