Why this matters

This isn't a vendor relationship. It's a mission match.

TRIO exists because access to higher education and economic opportunity should not be determined by where you were born or what your parents did for work. That belief is built into every federal dollar behind these programs.

AI Essentials exists for the same reason. We build sector-specific AI training for the populations that workforce training has historically left behind: first-generation students, adult learners returning to the workforce, low-income communities, and rural populations who never had access to the kind of career development that urban institutions take for granted.

When a TRIO program partners with AI Essentials, both organizations are doing the same work from different angles. One building the path, the other putting the tools in the hands of the people walking it.

"A first-generation student who graduates AI-fluent is more employable, more confident, and less likely to be displaced in the first wave of workforce AI disruption."

What a deployment looks like

From kickoff to cohort report in four weeks

Here is what a typical Student Support Services deployment looks like from start to finish. Every program type follows a similar structure, adapted to your cohort size and schedule.

1
Pre-assessment
All 40 students complete a sector-specific AI readiness baseline. Results give your program a clear picture of where each learner starts.
2
Cohort training
Students move through 12 hours of AI Essentials coursework in their selected track. Delivered in whatever format fits your program schedule.
3
Post-assessment
Every learner completes the post-assessment. Skills gain is calculated per student and aggregated for the cohort.
4
Credential and report
Students receive their AI-Ready certificate. You receive a cohort impact report with pre and post data ready for APR documentation.
