How it works

From a single classroom to a district-wide rollout

Most districts start with a pilot: one CTE pathway, one grade level, one cohort of 30 to 40 students. What they find is that the program runs cleanly inside their existing schedule, produces documentation their administrators can actually use, and generates enough student momentum that expansion becomes the natural next step.

We have deployed in 31 states across school districts ranging from a single rural high school to multi-campus urban systems. The model adapts. The outcome documentation is consistent.

1
Program selection
Your district selects which CTE pathway or grade level to pilot. We match it to the right AI Essentials track and configure the deployment for your schedule and delivery format.
2
Pre-assessment
Every student and participating teacher completes a sector-specific AI readiness baseline. Establishes a clear before picture for every learner in your cohort.
3
Learning cohort
12 hours of scenario-based AI training delivered in-person, hybrid, or fully online. Fits inside a semester elective, after-school program, or summer cohort without disrupting core instruction.
4
Credential and reporting
Students earn their AI-Ready sector certificate. Your district receives a cohort impact report with pre and post assessment data formatted for Perkins V and state reporting requirements.
